PUB01-Z06 · HOW IT WORKS

How it works

From structured answers to governed explanation

Assess

Complete the structured 73-question assessment.

Validate

Approved responses are normalized. Approved N/A responses are preserved only where C-01 v1.0.1 permits them; required multi-select responses cannot be empty, NONE/N/A is exclusive where provided, and Q73 does not permit N/A.

Analyse

C-02 v1.0.1 deterministic rules calculate seven domains and derived indicators, then select canonical drivers, confidence and eligible content. AI cannot change scores, classifications, drivers or null states.

Explain & Render

Governed AI turns approved explanation objects into clear language; web and PDF reports are produced from the same immutable report.
